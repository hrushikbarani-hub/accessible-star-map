Explore 144 bright named stars through a map, a list, or both. Read the coordinates. Follow a constellation. Keep a few favorites.

Lower magnitude means brighter. Coordinates use the catalog's J2000 epoch/equinox. This is an all-sky catalog chart; it does not calculate your current horizon, weather or daylight.

The whole celestial sphere

RA increases toward the left. Declination increases upward. Horizontal grid spacing is 6 hours; vertical spacing is 30 degrees. Map positions are schematic, not a view through a telescope. The list below provides the same catalog data.

Read the map in your own way.

Every plotted star has a matching list entry with its name, constellation, RA, declination and magnitude. You can explore entirely with keyboard or text. The map has no automatic animation, flashing or sound. Larger browser text and zoom remain supported.

This is a small bright-star selection, not a complete star atlas. Star names and positions come from the HYG catalog. Constellation descriptions are original summaries of the subset, and no official constellation boundaries are drawn.

Data: HYG 4.1 / Astronexus and catalog contributors. Named stars with HIP IDs and visual magnitude ≤3; selected, rounded and reformatted. Pinned source · CC BY-SA 4.0. The data subset retains that license; application code is MIT.
